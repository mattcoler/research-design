# Research Design

Research Design is a 5 EC course in the MSc Speech Technology at the University of Groningen, Campus Fryslân. Over seven teaching weeks you learn to formulate research questions, conduct systematic lit reviews, design sound methodologies and communicate your research, and you finish with a complete research proposal on a speech technology topic of your choice, a springboard for your master's thesis. Classes are seminars: we sit in a circle, work through structured activities and games, and give each other feedback along the way.

**BrightSpace is the authoritative source for the current year.** This site holds the course material that stays stable from year to year. Announcements, submissions, FeedbackFruits, grades and rooms are in BrightSpace, and if the two differ, BrightSpace wins.
