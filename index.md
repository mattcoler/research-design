## The course

Over seven weeks you turn an interest in speech technology into a research proposal you can take into your master's thesis. You learn to ask a focused research question, search and read the literature systematically, find a gap worth filling, and design a study that's ethical, feasible and repeatable. Then you explain it to specialists and non-specialists alike, in a blog post, a poster and a short talk.

## How it works

Classes are seminars. Most classes follow the same rhythm: a short introduction, two 30-minute activities with a break in between, and a wrap-up. Every activity relates to your own project, so each class moves your proposal forward.
