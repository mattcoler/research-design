# MERC: critical analysis of research papers

Critical appraisal is the systematic evaluation of research to judge trustworthiness, value, and relevance (Young & Solomon, 2009). The MERC framework, adapted from other critical appraisal methodologies (CASP, 2018), helps you evaluate research papers in speech tech. It asks four questions of each paper:

- **M**ethodology: are the research methods appropriate and rigorous for addressing the research question?
- **E**vidence: how strong is the evidence presented? Are claims supported by the data?
- **R**elevance: how does this work connect to your research question?
- **C**ontribution: what does this study add to the field?

We introduce MERC in Class 4 ({% include classdate.html n="4" fmt="medium" %}), and you use it in [Checkpoint 2]({{ '/assignments/checkpoint-2/' | relative_url }}), part 1. The MERC analysis worksheet, with space for your notes on each paper, is in BrightSpace. The questions on the worksheet are below.

## Paper details

- Citation
- Database/Source and date
- Your RQ

## Methodology

Are the research methods appropriate and rigorous for addressing the research question?

**Research design & appropriateness**

- Is the overall research design appropriate for the RQ?
- Are there methodological limitations that affect interpretation?

**Data collection & sampling**

- Are data collection methods clearly described and appropriate?
- Is the sample size adequate? Is it representative?
- Are potential confounding variables controlled?

**Speech technology-specific**

- Are evaluation metrics appropriate for the task?
- Is the baseline comparison fair and current?
- Are language/dialect considerations addressed?
- Do methods reflect real-world usage patterns?

## Evidence

How strong is the evidence presented? Are claims supported by the data?

**Statistical rigor**

- Are statistical tests appropriate and properly reported?
- Are effect sizes reported, as well as p-values?
- Is statistical power adequate?

**Claims vs. data**

- Are claims adequately supported by evidence?
- Are alternative explanations considered?
- Do authors acknowledge limitations appropriately?

**Generalizability**

- Can results generalize beyond the specific sample?
- Do computational results transfer to human performance?
- Are cross-linguistic claims supported by data?

## Relevance

How does this work connect to your research question?

**Connection to your research**

- How does this paper relate to your RQ? (direct/tangential/methodological)
- What aspects are most relevant to your investigation?
- What aspects are less relevant?

**Transferable insights**

- What methodological insights can you apply to your own work?
- What theoretical frameworks might inform your research?

## Contribution

What does this study add to the field?

**Nature of contribution**

- Significant (major advancement), incremental (modest step forward), or limited? Explain.
- What is novel about the approach or findings?

**Impact & limitations**

- What is the potential impact on the field?
- What limitations constrain the contribution?
- What future research directions does this suggest?

## Synthesis & comparative analysis

After analyzing 2+ papers, compare methodological approaches and identify patterns:

- How do different methodological approaches yield different types of insights?
- Where do studies complement or contradict each other?
- What theoretical insights emerge from considering both studies together?
