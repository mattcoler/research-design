# Frameworks

Four frameworks take you from question to gap. FARES tests your research question, PRISMA documents your search, MERC tests the papers you read, and TIPS tests the gap that connects them.

- [FARES]({{ '/frameworks/fares/' | relative_url }}): evaluating a research question (Class 2)
- [PRISMA]({{ '/frameworks/prisma/' | relative_url }}): documenting a literature search (Class 3)
- [MERC]({{ '/frameworks/merc/' | relative_url }}): reading papers critically (Class 4)
- [TIPS]({{ '/frameworks/tips/' | relative_url }}): evaluating a research gap (Class 5)

And for the reviews you give and receive: [peer review guidelines]({{ '/frameworks/peer-review/' | relative_url }}).
